# Brightline Logistics Employee Handbook

Brightline Logistics is a fictional company used for this demo. This handbook covers time off, remote work, expenses and equipment for all full-time employees in Canada and the United States.

## Time Off

### Vacation
New full-time employees receive 18 days of paid vacation per calendar year. After three years of service the allowance increases to 22 days, and after six years to 25 days. Vacation accrues monthly, at 1.5 days per month for new employees.

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used before March 31, otherwise they expire. Vacation requests longer than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance in the HR portal.

### Sick Leave
Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Unused sick days do not carry over.

### Parental Leave
Brightline tops up government parental benefits to 100% of base salary for the first 16 weeks of parental leave, for all parents regardless of gender.

### Public Holidays
The company observes all statutory holidays in the employee's province or state, plus one floating holiday per year that each employee can choose.

## Remote Work

Most roles are remote-first. Employees may work from anywhere in their country of employment. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year requires written approval from HR and Legal because of tax and data-protection rules.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern Time, Monday to Thursday. Fridays are meeting-free.

The company pays a home-office stipend of 600 USD (or 800 CAD) once when you join, and an internet allowance of 50 USD per month.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed through the Expensify app within 10 business days of approval. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD.

### Travel
- Flights: economy class for trips under 6 hours, premium economy for longer flights.
- Hotels: up to 220 USD per night in major cities, 160 USD elsewhere.
- Meals while travelling: a daily allowance of 75 USD; alcohol is not reimbursed.

### Learning Budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Requests above 500 USD need manager approval.

## Equipment

New employees choose a MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours (see the Security Policy).
